2. Two-Stage Retrieval Implementation
Pipeline 1 Baseline: encode 328 chunks, L2-normalize, FAISS IndexFlatIP for cosine (shape 328x384, index total 328). Per query encode, normalize, search Top 10. First 3 directly into the fixed prompt for Answer 1. Observed Top-10 cosine scores 0.29 to 0.40, lower and flatter than the NITI run (0.55 to 0.58) because the contract is JSON-schema heavy, which is exactly why re-ranking matters more here.
Pipeline 2 Re-Ranking: reuse same Top 10, score the 10 (query, chunk) pairs with Cross-Encoder ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2, sort descending, new Top 3 into the identical prompt for Answer 2. Only chunks differ. Same function structure as the NITI run: search_top10, make_prompt with strict answer-only-from-context plus say-not-found rule, get_answer1, rerank_top10, get_answer2.

3. Failure Mode Analysis
Scoring case 1, keyword trap on fees: query asked in plain words about convenience fees, true answer uses protocol terms misc fees at fulfillment level with title customized by BNP. p82_283 moved old 4 to new 1. Answer 1 had a chunk mentioning misc yet concluded the title-text part was not found, so incomplete. Answer 2 complete: misc convenience fees at fulfillment level, title customized by BNP. Omission fixed.
Scoring case 2, new-retailer exception precision gain: general rule needs distributor mapping, exception is that a group with type new can already order. Promotion p3_9 old 4 to new 2 brought mapping-declaration detail into Top 3. Answer 1 was a bare yes with one clause, Answer 2 grounded it in protocol steps with Seller NP picking distributor, creation pending and P1 naming. Degrade to restore.
Supporting buried-promotion cases with both answers correct: error codes 50015 declined and 50016 lapsed with 10 to 2 and 9 to 3, provider-id omission NACK 30000 with 6 to 1 and 7 to 3, distributor must-buy rule with 2 to 1, feature codes 030 and 031 with 4 to 1 and 6 to 2, retailer-mapping declarations with 4 to 1 and 5 to 3.
Honest limitations: a tax-display question went from partial Answer 1 to Not found Answer 2, the first convenience-fee phrasing scored Not found both ways, and one declarations answer came out terser after re-ranking. Re-ranking promotes keyword-dense chunks, which usually helps but can narrow the answer. All scoring cases satisfy buried 4-10 promoted to Top 3.

4. Analysis and Report
4.1 Architecture. Bi-Encoder encodes query and chunk independently, then cosine; vectors precomputed once, fast, but no query-chunk interaction, so it fails on synonyms (convenience fees vs misc) and misses exception clauses sitting at ranks 4 to 10. Cross-Encoder encodes each (query, chunk) pair jointly with self-attention and scores relevance directly; no precompute, slow, but context-aware. Hence retrieve-then-rerank: Top 10 fast, careful read of 10 only.
4.2 Qualitative and rank shift. Shifts logged: 4 to 1, 8 to 2, 8 to 1, 5 to 3, 4 to 2, 6 to 1, 7 to 3, 10 to 2, 9 to 3, 2 to 1. The strict prompt kept hallucinations out (models said not found instead of inventing); observed faults were omission of the title-text clause and thin exception answers, both restored by promotion of gold chunks into the Top 3.
4.3 Latency (Colab T4, mean of 5 for retrieval, single end-to-end timing). Baseline Top 10: 0.0075 s. Re-rank of 10 pairs extra: 0.0437 s (about 6x baseline). Retrieval total with re-ranking: about 0.051 s. Pipeline 1 with LLM: 0.951 s. Pipeline 2 with LLM: 0.732 s. Pipeline 2 measured faster this run, which is LLM variance; the stable finding is that retrieval overhead is small and LLM time dominates and varies. Same pattern as the NITI run.
4.4 Scaling. Cross-Encoder needs a full forward pass per pair with nothing precomputable. 10 pairs cost about 44 ms here, so all 328 chunks would cost about 1.4 s per query and lakhs of chunks would take hours. Bi-Encoder vectors are precomputed with fast FAISS search. So prefilter to 10 then re-rank gives speed plus accuracy.
